The studio

A small studio with an unusual rule

Imalive started in 2021 as two people who were tired of shipping apps that broke on real phones. We adopted one rule early and never dropped it: nothing goes to a store listing until it passes the release gate. No exceptions, no "we will fix it in the first patch".

That rule cost us clients at first. It also gave us a 99.97% crash-free rate and a 4.8 average store rating across everything we have shipped, which turns out to be a better sales pitch than speed.

What we make

We build our own products first, because it keeps us honest. Imalive is our check-in app, born from a co-founder living alone during a bad winter. Janus is our decision journal, born from a stack of notebooks full of calls that were never reviewed. Both are live, both are maintained by the same people who built them.

How we take on client work

We run one client engagement at a time. That means you get the whole studio, not a fraction of an agency. We take a scoping call, quote a fixed price, and if the fit is wrong we say so before you spend anything.

Values

  • Testable or it does not count. Every claim about quality has a test behind it.
  • No dark patterns. Cancel is as easy as subscribe, and data exports are real files.
  • Small surface area. Fewer features, finished properly, beats a long feature list.
  • Say the hard thing early. Bad news on day three is cheap. On day sixty it is not.

FAQ

Questions we get asked

What is imaliveapp.com?

imaliveapp.com is the website of Imalive Labs LLC, an independent app studio based in Albuquerque, New Mexico. We build and test mobile apps, and we publish our own products: the Imalive check-in app and the Janus decision journal.

What does Imalive actually do?

You tap one button once a day. Your chosen contacts see that you checked in. If you miss a day, Imalive reminds you; miss two and it escalates to your contacts. It is designed for people living alone, solo travellers, and families keeping an eye on each other.

What is Janus?

A decision journal. You record a decision and what you expect to happen. Janus follows up later and asks how it went, then scores how well you predicted it. Over time it shows you where your judgement is strong and where it is not.

How much do the apps cost?

Both apps have a free tier. Imalive Plus is $4.99 per month and Imalive Family is $9.99 per month. Janus Pro is $6.99 per month. Annual billing is available at roughly two months free. All prices are in USD.

How do I pay, and who processes it?

All payments are handled by Stripe. We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Apple Pay. We never see or store your full card number.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from the account screen in the app, or email billing@imaliveapp.com. Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

What is the refund policy?

Full refund within 14 days of any charge, no reason needed, and unused annual time is refunded pro rata. Full details are on our refund policy page.

Do you sell my data?

No. We do not sell or rent personal data. We use a small set of processors for hosting, payments, and crash reporting. Details are in the privacy policy.

Can I export my data?

Yes, from both apps, as Markdown and CSV. Ask support if you want a full account export and we will send it within five business days.

Do you build apps for other companies?

Yes, on a limited basis. We run one client engagement at a time. A launch audit is $2,400 one-time; full builds start at $14,000. Start with a scoping call through the contact page.

Which platforms do you support?

The apps run on iOS 16+ and Android 11+. Imalive also has a web check-in page.

How do I reach support?

Email hello@imaliveapp.com for anything, or billing@imaliveapp.com for payment matters. Phone support is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm Mountain.
